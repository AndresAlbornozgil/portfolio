Rivet Reactor

Rivet Reactor was conceived as a compact energy display system for field diagnostics, crafted to feel like a relic from a machine age while still functioning with clarity and precision.

The concept resolved around modular control surfaces, durable metal forms, and a measured visual rhythm that made operation feel intentional and calm.

Build Notes

  • CAD-driven layout
  • Brass and steel fabrication
  • Thermal response testing
