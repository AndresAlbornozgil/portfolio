Chronometer Dock

Chronometer Dock was developed as a tactile command surface for planning, measuring, and observing mechanism behavior in a refined workshop context.

The design emphasizes crafted utility, with cast-metal framing, precise control placement, and a sense of historical permanence without losing modern clarity.

Build Notes

  • Workshop control station
  • Brass detailing and assembly
  • Interface and planning system
